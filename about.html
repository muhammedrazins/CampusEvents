About the Campus Event & Workshop Portal

Welcome to the official Student Technical Workshop and Seminar Registration System. This portal streamlines seat reservations for departmental bootcamps, hands-on hardware sessions, and guest lectures across campus.

1. Registration Rules

Each student may register once per workshop using their official University Roll Number and email address. Duplicate entries are automatically blocked by the server.

2. Seat Capacity & Passes

Workshops have strict laboratory or seminar hall seating caps. Once your form is validated, an instant Digital Pass ID (PASS-XXXXXX) is generated.

3. Entry Verification

Bring your printed or saved PDF pass to the venue 15 minutes prior to the session. Coordinators will verify your Pass ID against the Admin Dashboard.
